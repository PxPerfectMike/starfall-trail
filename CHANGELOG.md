# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Follow a party member: pick someone in your group and the arrow and the trail lead you to them
  instead of to a quest. Pick them from the minimap menu, by right-clicking them, or with
  `/sft friend <name>`; `/sft friend off` goes back to quests.
- The choice is dropped when they leave your group.
- The minimap button now starts at the bottom of the minimap on new installs.
- `/sft probe party` reports the positions the game gives for group members.

## 1.0.0 (first public release)

- Ground trail of sparkles along a walkable route for all of Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms.
- 3D arrow that follows the route and turns with the camera.
- Text under the arrow says what to do ("Kill 7 Thistle Boar", "Turn in to ...").
- Kill and collect objectives count as an area: the arrow and the trail fade out there and the text moves up.
- Minimap button with toggles for the trail, the arrow and the text.
- Auto focus on the closest quest; corpse runs.
